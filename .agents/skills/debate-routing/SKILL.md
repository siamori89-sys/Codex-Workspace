# DEBATE ROUTING

Honor an explicit current-turn level or persistent level before automatic classification. Use DIRECT for non-substantive operational turns; LIGHT for limited explanations; STANDARD for meaningful analysis; DEEP for complex multi-step issues. Choose MAX for a proof, logical argument, reasoned agreement, objection, disagreement, contradiction claim, counterexample, challenge to an assumption or prior conclusion, important argument modification, difficult entailment question, or central interconnected argument. When substantive-logic classification is uncertain, choose higher effort.
